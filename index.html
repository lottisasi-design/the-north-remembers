CHAPTER I

The Man
of Winter

There is something about you that is difficult to describe.

The quiet confidence, the mysterious nature, the way you act as though nothing affects you.

A little bit of Jon Snow, perhaps. A man who says very little but whose actions often say everything.

CHAPTER II

What Winter
Hides

People might see the tough exterior. The attitude. The silence.

But there is another side to you. The one that cares, listens, protects and shows up when it matters.

Sometimes the warmest hearts belong to the people who pretend to be cold.

02
CHAPTER III

The Warrior
With an Attitude

The teasing, the arguments, the occasional attitude and those moments when you act like you don't care.

Somehow, all of it has become part of what makes you, you.

And honestly, I wouldn't want this story to have a different character playing your part.

CHAPTER V

The Unspoken
Truth

Not everything meaningful needs to be said out loud.

Some bonds live in the little things: the conversations, the laughter, the support and the comfort of knowing someone is there.

You may never realise how much those little things mean.

CHAPTER VI

The Next
Chapter

Life will keep moving. There will be new places, new people and new stories.

But I hope you never forget that somewhere, someone will always be cheering for you.

Keep being the person you are. Keep winning your battles.

A PERSONAL NOTE

A Few Words
I Owe You

There is something I have wanted to tell you.

Telisi chesina thappulaina, teliyaka chesina thappulaina... naa valla eppudaina meeru hurt ayyi unte, I am genuinely, very, very sorry. Maybe sometimes I don't express things properly. Maybe I make mistakes without even realising them. But hurting you was never my intention.

And one thing I want you to remember — no one could ever be you. Not your way of thinking, not your presence, not the way you care, and definitely not the person you are.

The way I love you, admire you, respect you and look up to you today will never change. Not with time, not with distance, and not with circumstances.

Some people are irreplaceable.
You are one of them.

The North remembers. And so will I. 07
